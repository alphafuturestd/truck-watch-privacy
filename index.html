Motion Monitor Beta

Application: Motion Monitor Beta
Developer: Alpha Future Studio
Effective date: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Alpha Future Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Motion Monitor Beta ("the App") accesses, processes, stores, and handles information when you use our Android application.

2. Camera Access and Image Processing

The App requires access to your device's camera to provide its core motion detection functionality.

  • Camera images are processed locally on your device.
  • Camera frames are analyzed exclusively for motion detection.
  • Camera images and videos are not recorded or permanently stored by the App.
  • Camera data is not uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.
  • The camera is used only while the App is actively performing motion detection with the screen enabled.

You can revoke camera permission at any time through your device settings.

3. App Permissions and Notifications

The current version of the App asks for two device permissions:

  • Camera: used to analyze motion in the monitoring area selected by the user. Camera frames are processed locally as described above.
  • Notifications: used to display motion detection alerts and relevant App status notifications on your device.

Notifications are generated and displayed locally by the App. Granting notification permission does not give us access to your messages, contacts, or other notifications, and the App does not send the content of these alerts to our servers.

You can manage or revoke either permission through Android settings. Without camera access, motion detection cannot operate. If notifications are disabled, you may not receive the App's notification alerts.

4. Information We Collect

The App does not require user registration or account creation.

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location through the App.

The App does not collect or transmit camera images or videos to external servers.

If you contact us for support, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, including your email address and the contents of your message. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

5. Local Data Storage

The App may store certain preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Motion detection sensitivity.
  • Selected monitoring area.
  • Alarm settings.
  • Other application preferences.

These settings are used to provide and maintain the App's functionality.

We do not operate a remote database containing these settings.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The App does not sell personal information.

The App does not share camera images, recorded videos, or motion detection data with third parties.

The current version does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

The App is distributed through Google Play, which may independently process information in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

7. Data Security

We seek to protect user privacy by processing camera information locally and minimizing the information handled by the App.

The App does not transmit camera images to remote servers.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Camera frames are processed temporarily for motion detection and are not permanently retained by the App.

Application preferences may remain stored locally until they are cleared or the App is uninstalled, subject to Android's backup and restoration settings.

Support correspondence may be retained only as necessary to respond to requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request deletion of information that you have voluntarily provided through support communications.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, its features, data handling practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
